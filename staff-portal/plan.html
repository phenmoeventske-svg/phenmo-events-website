Install Phenmo Staff App

Standalone application for phones & computers

Checking device...

No app store download required • Zero installation fee

1

Open this portal in Google Chrome or Samsung Internet on your Android phone.

2

Tap the "📲 Install Now" button above, or tap the Chrome menu (⋮ three dots) and select "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").

3

The Phenmo Staff icon will appear on your home screen and app drawer. Tap it to launch full-screen anytime!

1

Open this portal in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome on your Windows PC or laptop.

2

Click the "📲 Install Now" button above, or click the Install icon (computer monitor with down arrow, or + icon) in the right side of the browser address bar.

3

Click "Install". Phenmo Staff opens in its own clean window and pins directly to your Windows Taskbar and Start Menu!

1

Open this portal in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.

2

Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (the square with an arrow pointing upward ⎋).

3

Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen", then tap "Add" in the top-right corner. It installs right on your iPhone home screen!

1

Open this portal in Google Chrome or Safari on your Mac.

2

In Chrome: click the Install icon in the URL address bar. In Safari: choose File → Add to Dock.

3

Phenmo Staff will run as a native Mac window pinned in your Dock!
